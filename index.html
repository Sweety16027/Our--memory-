To My Thangoo ❤️

A little collection of memories from me

To My Thangoo 🫂 ❤️

First of all, thank you for coming into my life 🫂.

I honestly don’t know how to explain what you mean to me. I have received love, care and support from my parents and my sister, and I’m truly grateful for that. But receiving this kind of love and care from someone ✨ special in my life feels different. Maybe that is why you became so special to me 🤗 .

I still remember the first time I met you in Bangalore. Even then, I liked you ❤️ . It started as a little 🥰 . I used to secretly look at you whenever I got the chance 😉 . I wanted to talk to you, but at the same time, I was scared and confused 🤔. I knew there could be struggles because of our families, and there were already so many things for me to think about 😞 .

But somehow, I feel like God had a plan for us 🙏.

When you messaged me in April 🗓️ , I was genuinely happy 😊. From that little conversation, we slowly started talking more and more. Without even realizing it, talking to you became something I started looking forward to.

When you first asked me to meet you, I was scared too 😐 . But I still said yes. And honestly, our first meeting didn’t go the way I imagined 💭 . There was tension 😨 , and we ended up fighting. I felt bad about it for the whole day 🥺 . I’m sorry for that.

But after that, something changed beautifully 🤗 .

You started caring for me more. You gave me so much love and support, and you made me feel safe being myself 🙂 . And slowly, without even realizing it, my love for you became stronger 💯 .

Even now, I have one little fear in my heart — the fear of missing you too much 🥺 .

I really like you, Thangam. ❤️ Please take care of yourself and take care of me too 🫂 .

We have met only five times , but every meeting means so much to me 😍. You have travelled so much just to see me. Whether you came by bus or by bike, whether you were tired or travelling wasn’t convenient for you, you still came because you wanted to see me 😣 .

That means more to me than you probably realize.

Sometimes I think, “We aren’t even living in the same place, but this person is still doing so much just for me 😞 .”

If you were here in Chennai, maybe I wouldn’t understand the value of it this much. I would probably think, “He’s nearby, he can come and see me.” But you are in Bangalore and I’m here, and still you travel 🚌 all that distance just to spend some time with me.

That makes me feel incredibly special 🥲 .

And I know you have your own pain and tiredness 🥱 , but you don’t always show it to me. Instead, you tell me things like, “It’s okay 👍 , don’t overthink. I’m fine.” Sometimes you even say that seeing me makes you forget the pain 😊 .

That kind of love stays in my heart ❤️ .

You are genuinely special to me 🥰 .

I have met and known many people, but I have never felt this kind of comfort with anyone else. You don’t try to change me. You don’t put unnecessary restrictions on me. You don’t give me a list of rules about how I should be.

You simply accept me as I am 🫂 .

And for that, I will always be thankful 🙏 .

From the beginning of April 🗓️ until now, I still feel the same way about you. I don’t feel like I have to become someone else around you. Thank you for accepting me exactly as I am 😘 .

You take care of me in so many ways. Sometimes it feels like you are there for me in every role I need — not because you have to, but because you genuinely care.

And there is one thing I especially love about us.

No matter how much we fight 😤 , you never let the fight stay between us for too long.

Sometimes within five or ten minutes, you come back and talk to me again 🥲 .

You ask me, “Did you eat?”
“Did you sleep?”
“be safe .”
“Don’t do works take rest.”

Those little things may sound simple, but they mean so much to me 🤗 .

People say that when couples fight 😤, they might slowly drift apart. But with you, even after a fight, you still come back and care about me.

That is something I will never take for granted.

I love you so much pa .

All I want is for you to stay beside me and take care of yourself and me. Even when I make mistakes, knowingly or unknowingly, I hope you won’t give up on me easily ☺️ .

That is enough for me 🤗 .

I want you in my life for a long, long time 🫂🫂🫂 .

Sometimes I imagine a future where we can simply spend time together, talk for hours, laugh about silly things, watch something together, and enjoy the little moments without worrying about anything else.

I want to be able to see you whenever I miss you 🥺 .

Our video calls are also some of my favourite memories 😍😍 . Even when you are busy with work, if you have only five minutes, ten minutes, or even two minutes, you still make time for me.

And when you say, “I’ll call 🤙 you in two minutes,” you actually call.

Those two minutes may seem small to someone else, but to me, they mean a lot because they show me that you remember me even when you are busy 😘 .

I understand that there will be days when you cannot talk to me because you have work or other things going on. I don’t expect you to be available all the time.

But knowing that you still make an effort to spend whatever little time you can with me makes me happy 😊 .

You take such good care of me, and I keep saying this because I genuinely mean it.

Because of you, I feel happy 😊 .

Even when things at home become difficult, even when I feel stressed or lonely, talking to you somehow makes everything feel a little lighter.

Sometimes I may feel alone, but I know that I can come to you and talk.

Whether I’m at the office, outside, or at home, when something hurts me or when I simply feel lonely, talking to you gives me comfort ✨ .

And that comfort is something very precious to me 💫 .

Thank you for being that person for me 👩‍❤️‍👨 .

Thank you for every message 💌 , every call 🤙 , every meeting 👩‍❤️‍👨 , every little effort, every sacrifice, every “Did you eat?”, every “Sleep well”, and every time you came back to talk to me after a fight.

Maybe you don’t realize how much those little things mean to me.

But I do.

And I will always remember them.

I love you so much, Thangam. ❤️

Please stay the same person you are ☺️ .

Please take care of yourself 🥺 .

And whenever life gets difficult, remember that there is someone who genuinely values you, appreciates you, and feels lucky to have you in her life 🫂 .

Thank you for coming into my life ❤️ .
Thank you for staying 👩‍❤️‍👨 .
And thank you for being you. ❤️

Always yours,
[Sweetheart 🤞]
